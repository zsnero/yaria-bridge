Yaria Bridge

Link this browser to the Yaria desktop app.

Desktop connection

Yaria Bridge sends download jobs to the Yaria app on your computer. Enable Bridge in Yaria Settings and paste the pairing token below.

How it works

  1. Open the Yaria desktop app.
  2. Go to Settings → Bridge and turn integration on.
  3. Copy the pairing token into this page.
  4. On any video page, open Yaria Bridge, pick quality, and download.
  5. The job appears on Yaria’s Downloads page.
